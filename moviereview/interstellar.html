Interstellar

★★★★★

In the future, Earth is becoming uninhabitable due to environmental changes and crop failures. Former NASA pilot Cooper joins a team of astronauts who travel through a newly discovered wormhole near Saturn to search for a habitable planet. As he travels through space, Cooper must face difficult choices involving time, family, love, and humanity's survival.

Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure, Drama

Release Year: 2014

Director: Christopher Nolan
