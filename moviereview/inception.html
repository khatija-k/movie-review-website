Inception

★★★★★

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal valuable information from their subconscious. He is offered a chance to return to his children if he can successfully perform an unusual task called inception, planting an idea inside someone's mind. As the mission progresses, the boundaries between dreams and reality become increasingly difficult to distinguish.

Genre: Sci-Fi, Action, Thiller

Release Year: 2010

Director: Christopher Nolan
