Elemental

★★★★☆

In Element City, residents made of fire, water, earth, and air live together, although they generally remain separated by their differences. Ember, a passionate young fire resident, meets Wade, a kind and emotional water resident. Their unexpected relationship challenges Ember's beliefs about life, family expectations, and whether people from completely different worlds can truly connect.

Genre: Animation, Comdey, Romance

Release Year: 2023

Director: Peter Sohn
