Avatar: The Way of Water

★★★★☆

More than a decade after the events of the first film, Jake Sully and Neytiri are raising their family on Pandora. When the human forces return, the family is forced to leave their home and seek refuge with the Metkayina, a water-based Na'vi clan. Jake and his family must adapt to their new environment while protecting each other.

Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure, Fantasy

Release Year: 2022

Director: James Cameron
